A number is divisible by 4 if the last two digits from a number divisible by 4.
A number is divisible by 8 if the last three digits form a number divisible by 8.
A number is divisible by 3 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 3.
A number is divisible by 9 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 9.
A number is divisible by 6 if it is divisible by both 2 and 3.
A number is divisible by 12 if it is divisible by both 4 and 3.
If a number is divisible by 7, you can check by doubling the last digit, subtracting that from the remaining part of the number, and if the result is a multiple of 7 or zero, then the original number is divisible by 7.

If the difference of the sum of alternative digits of a number is divisible by 11, then that number is divisible by 11 completely.
Sum of digits in odd places – Sum of digits in even places = 0 or a multiple of 11.

Concept of Co-Prime numbers
Co-prime numbers are pairs of numbers that don't share any common factors except for 1.
To be considered co-prime, a set of numbers must have at least two numbers, and the only common factor between them should be 1.

The Highest Common Factor (HCF) of co-prime numbers always is 1.
The Lowest Common Multiples (LCM) of co-prime numbers always equals the product of numbers.


Power Cycle Type:

Digits 0, 1, 5, and 6:

52 = 25: Unit digit is 5, the number itself.
15 = 1: Unit digit is 1, the number itself.
09 = 0: Unit digit is 0, the number itself.
62 = 36: Unit digit is 6, the number itself.

Digits 4 and 9:
Both of these two digits, 4 and 9, have a cyclicity of two different digits as their unit digit.

4odd = 4: If 4 is raised to the power of an odd number, the unit digit will be 4.
4even = 6: If 4 is raised to the power of an even number, the unit digit will be 6.
9odd = 9: If 9 is raised to the power of an odd number, the unit digit will be 9.
9even = 1: If 9 is raised to the power of an even number, the unit digit will be 1.

Digits 2, 3, 7, and 8:

The cyclicity of 3 has 4 different numbers: 3, 9, 7, 1.
The cyclicity of 7 has 4 different numbers: 7, 9, 3, 1.
The cyclicity of 8 has 4 different numbers: 8, 4, 2, 6.
